Consilience QuickBooks Integration — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the "Consilience" QuickBooks Online integration ("the App"), operated by Keystone Consilience (Pty) Ltd (CIPC registration 2016/0008434/07), Pretoria, South Africa, handles data. The App is a private, internal tool used only by Keystone Consilience to access its own QuickBooks Online company data. It is not offered to third parties or external end users.

1. Data we access

Through Intuit's official QuickBooks Online Accounting API, the App can read and write the company's own accounting records — including customers, vendors, invoices, bills, accounts, items, and financial reports. The App accesses only the QuickBooks company that an authorised administrator has explicitly connected.

2. How data is used

Accounting data is used solely to carry out the internal user's immediate request (for example, retrieving a report or creating an invoice) and to generate a response. We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

3. AI processing and sub-processors

The App operates through Anthropic's Claude assistant. To fulfil a request, the relevant QuickBooks data is transmitted securely to Anthropic for that single inference only. This data is not used to train any AI model and is not retained for any purpose beyond serving the request. Anthropic is our only sub-processor for this purpose.

4. Data storage and security

The App does not maintain a separate database of QuickBooks business data. OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the operator's own machine in a file with restricted permissions. All communication with Intuit and Anthropic occurs over encrypted TLS/HTTPS connections.

5. Data sharing

We do not share QuickBooks data with any party other than the AI model provider for the single inference described above. Data is used only for the benefit of the original company (ourselves).

6. Data retention and deletion

No business data is retained by the App beyond the lifetime of a request. Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the App in the QuickBooks company settings, which invalidates stored tokens.

7. Your rights (POPIA)

As a South African entity, we handle personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Because the App processes only our own company's data for our own internal operations, no external data subjects' information is collected through the App.
